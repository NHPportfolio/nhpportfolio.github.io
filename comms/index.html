Selected Work

Achievement Network · Office of the CEO · 2018–2020

Rolling Out a New Staff Intranet

Need
A national education nonprofit of about 230 staff, most working remotely or in schools, was launching a new staff intranet.
Did
I ran a communications audit and staff listening tour first. Then I wrote the launch messaging, training guides, and leader talking points around what I heard, and followed up on staff feedback after launch.
Result
Every employee adopted it.

USAID · International · 2012–2015

Reaching Frontline Audiences After a Disaster

Need
A $20M post-earthquake infrastructure and resettlement project had to keep residents, crews, and officials informed while construction disrupted daily life.
Did
I gave residents and crews advance notice of disruptions through printed notices, radio, and SMS. I ran community meetings and site briefings, wrote talking points for USAID and Embassy officials, and brought residents' concerns back to the USAID mission. I also redesigned the project's reporting and trained 8 engineers and project managers to run it.
Result
Reporting delays fell 80%, alongside three years of direct communication in two languages.

WABE, NPR and PBS Atlanta · Culture Committee

Employee Engagement Inside a Public Media Organization

Need
Leadership wanted a clear read on staff culture and satisfaction, and a way to act on it.
Did
As co-chair, I met monthly with the CEO and HR, ran culture focus groups, built and sent org-wide surveys, and planned quarterly all-staff events.
Result
I organized a pilot of Idea Science, a human-centered design workshop run with Stoked, for 12 staff from across the organization.

Harper's Magazine · 2015–2018
